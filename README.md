# Hi, I'm Tanvi 👋

This is the GitHub home for my portfolio. I'm a product designer studying Applied Cognition and Neuroscience with a focus on Human Computer Interaction, and this site is where I keep my case studies, a little about me, and a lot of travel.

I built this portfolio around the trips I keep taking and the boarding passes I refuse to throw away: projects are boarding passes, my skills are packed luggage tags, and the About page opens like a passport. Most of the artwork, like the beach on the homepage and the flowers, is hand drawn by me.

## What's inside

- **Case studies:** the Tango Portal template redesign and reward campaigns from my internship at Blackhawk Network, and a Delta Airlines booking redesign.
- **About me:** my background, where I've been, and what I'm looking for next.
- **A postcard:** the contact form at the bottom of the page, if you'd like to say hello.

It's built with React, Vite and Tailwind CSS.
